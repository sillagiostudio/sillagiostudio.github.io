Common questions

How do I reorder my collection?
Hold a fragrance and drag it, or tap Edit. You can also swipe right on a fragrance and choose Move to send it to a numbered position.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Yes, through your own iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure iCloud is turned on for Sillagio. Changes can take a minute to appear.

How does the membership work?
Everything is free for your first 6 months. After that, an annual membership keeps editing on. Without one, you can still view and share everything you've added. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a membership on a new device, open Settings in Sillagio and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I add notes?
In a fragrance's form, type them into Top, Middle and Base, or copy notes from anywhere and tap Paste. Sillagio sorts them into the right layers.

Why does it ask for my location?
Only to check the weather for "What should I wear?". It's optional, approximate and never stored.

Can I export my data?
Yes. In Settings, Export to CSV creates files you can open in Numbers or Excel, and Import from CSV reads them back.
